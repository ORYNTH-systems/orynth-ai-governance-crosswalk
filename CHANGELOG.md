## [1.0.0] — 2026-07-30 — Public Proof Reference

### Summary

Version 1.0.0 is the first public release of the ORYNTH AI Governance Crosswalk. It evaluates 34 atomic requirements drawn from Executive Order 14409 and Minnesota HF 1606 / Chapter 72 against publicly indexed mechanisms in the ORYNTH Reference Architecture v1.0.0 and Architectural Audit Specification v1.0.0.

### Methodology

The adjudication pipeline applies a deterministic, adversarially reviewed methodology across seven stages: primary-source extraction, requirement normalization, taxonomy mapping, evidence indexing, threshold adjudication, adversarial review, and release freeze. Every verdict traces to a primary government record.

### Evidence

24 of 40 indexed public evidence records were used, establishing 66 requirement-to-evidence relationships. 32 residual duties are preserved.

### Claim boundary

This release is an architectural compatibility assessment. It does not establish legal or regulatory compliance, government approval, regulatory certification, implementation certification, safety, or correctness.

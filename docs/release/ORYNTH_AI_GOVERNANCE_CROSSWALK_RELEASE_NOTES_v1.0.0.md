### Methodology and Traceability
The release applies a deterministic, adversarially reviewed methodology. Every verdict traces to a primary government record. The canonical row-level matrix (`data/public/ORYNTH_AI_GOVERNANCE_CROSSWALK.csv`) serves as the single source of truth for all counts and statistics.

### Limitations and Boundaries
- The corpus is selected and bounded; it does not claim complete federal or 50-state coverage.
- Compatibility findings are strictly architectural, not legal.
- The 13 identified gaps are preserved as valid research results.
- Independent implementations are not certified.
- Safety and correctness are not established or guaranteed.
- External legal, operational, and governmental duties remain preserved.

# August 1, 2026 — Preliminary Determination

## Federal Executive Order 14409

Executive Order 14409 was signed and publicly issued on June 2, 2026.

The August 1, 2026 date arises from provisions requiring federal officials
to complete specified actions within 60 days.

The preliminary requirement classes include:

- classified cyber-capability benchmarking
- covered-frontier-model classification
- voluntary developer consultation
- controlled governmental access processes
- confidentiality and security safeguards

The August 1 deadline must not be represented as a universal compulsory
submission deadline for all AI companies unless later legislation,
regulation, contract terms, or binding agency action creates that duty.

## Minnesota HF 1606

Minnesota HF 1606 concerns access to nudification technology.

The measure became enacted law in 2026 and carries an August 1, 2026
effective date.

Unlike the federal executive-order implementation deadline, the Minnesota
law creates direct restrictions applicable to covered private actors.

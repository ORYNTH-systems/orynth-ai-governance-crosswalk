# EO 14409 — Authority Boundary Analysis

## Mandatory Federal action

The order binds identified executive-branch actors through repeated use of
the term "shall."

## Voluntary private participation

The AI cybersecurity clearinghouse uses voluntary collaboration with the AI
industry and critical-infrastructure operators.

The frontier-model framework must be designed as a voluntary framework with
AI developers.

## Conditional pre-release access

The provision allowing developers to provide access to covered frontier
models for up to 30 days before release to trusted partners exists inside
the voluntary framework.

It is not expressed as a universal duty imposed on all developers.

## Prohibited construction

Section 3 does not authorize mandatory governmental:

- licensing;
- preclearance; or
- permitting

for the development, publication, release, or distribution of new AI
models, including frontier models.

## Legal-effect boundary

The order:

- preserves existing statutory agency authority;
- remains subject to applicable law and appropriations; and
- creates no privately enforceable right or benefit.

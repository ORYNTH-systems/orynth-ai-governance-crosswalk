# EO 14409 — Operative Analysis

## Controlling distinction

Executive Order 14409 creates mandatory duties for identified Federal
officials and Federal bodies.

It does not create a universal August 1, 2026 submission requirement for AI
companies.

## July 2, 2026 deadline

The 30-day provisions require Federal action concerning:

- National Security Systems cyber defense;
- Department of War cyber defense;
- civilian Federal system directives and guidance;
- AI-enabled Federal cybersecurity programs;
- access to cybersecurity tools and services;
- formation of an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse; and
- review of relevant Federal grant funding.

## August 1, 2026 deadline

The 60-day provisions require Federal officials to:

- expand cybersecurity-specialist hiring and placement pathways;
- develop and maintain classified AI cyber-capability benchmarks;
- determine the threshold for covered-frontier-model designation; and
- design a voluntary engagement framework with AI developers.

## Private-sector status

AI-developer participation under section 3(b) is voluntary.

Participating developers may:

- consult the Federal Government concerning model designation;
- provide controlled pre-release access to covered frontier models; and
- collaborate in selecting trusted early-access partners.

## Authority limitation

Section 3(c) prevents section 3 from being construed as authority to create
mandatory governmental licensing, preclearance, or permitting for new AI
models.

## Determination

A statement that all AI companies are required to provide models to the
Federal Government by August 1, 2026 would be inaccurate under the order's
text alone.

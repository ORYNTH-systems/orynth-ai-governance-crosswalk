# United States AI Governance Timeline, 2019–2026

## 2019–2022 — Study and inventory

Early state activity emphasized:

- task forces;
- advisory commissions;
- state-system inventories;
- facial-recognition limits;
- government procurement;
- automated-decision studies.

## 2023–2024 — Sector and synthetic-media expansion

The legislative field expanded into:

- election deepfakes;
- synthetic sexual media;
- voice and likeness;
- employment;
- education;
- healthcare;
- consumer disclosure.

## 2024–2025 — Comprehensive governance

States began assigning duties directly to developers and deployers involving:

- reasonable care;
- risk-management programs;
- impact assessments;
- notice;
- documentation;
- anti-discrimination;
- attorney-general enforcement.

## 2025–2026 — Frontier capability and infrastructure

The current phase includes:

- frontier-model classification;
- cyber-capability benchmarking;
- model-access safeguards;
- compute and infrastructure;
- companion chatbots;
- prohibited capabilities;
- model-provider accountability;
- data-center energy and water impacts.

## Temporal conclusion

United States AI governance has evolved from studying systems, to regulating
specific harms, to assigning lifecycle obligations, and finally toward
governing model capability and infrastructure.

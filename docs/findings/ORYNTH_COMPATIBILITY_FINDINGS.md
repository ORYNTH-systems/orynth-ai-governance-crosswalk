# ORYNTH Compatibility Findings

## Determination

ORYNTH demonstrates strongest alignment where governance requirements concern:

- authority and responsibility boundaries;
- execution admission;
- execution restriction;
- evidence classification;
- evidence preservation;
- derivation chains;
- validation;
- reproducibility;
- bounded conformance assessment.

## Federal findings

ORYNTH materially supports the architectural governance of:

- benchmarking processes;
- model classification;
- controlled model access;
- evidence protection;
- authority preservation;
- scope limitation.

ORYNTH does not supply Federal institutions, cybersecurity operations,
classified benchmark content, staffing, grants, or criminal enforcement.

## Minnesota findings

ORYNTH supports architectural restriction of externally classified prohibited
actions.

ORYNTH does not independently supply nudification detection, statutory
interpretation, advertising controls, civil remedies, penalties, courts, or
attorney-general authority.

## Governing conclusion

ORYNTH can operationalize externally defined authority, eligibility, evidence,
and execution restrictions.

It does not replace legislation or establish legal compliance.

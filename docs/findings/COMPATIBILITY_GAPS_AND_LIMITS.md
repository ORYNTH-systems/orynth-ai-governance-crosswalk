# Compatibility Gaps and Limits

## Direct public architectural gap

The current corpus identifies one direct public architectural gap:

- advertising and promotional-distribution control.

## Domain modules still required

External domain-specific mechanisms remain necessary for:

- frontier-model cyber benchmarks;
- vulnerability scanning;
- nudification detection;
- technical-skill classification;
- identity-harm classification;
- statutory notice;
- domain-specific risk metrics.

## Outside-scope functions

ORYNTH does not create:

- government agencies;
- courts;
- penalties;
- causes of action;
- attorney-general authority;
- appropriations;
- grant programs;
- sovereign enforcement;
- government staffing.

These are outside architectural scope, not ORYNTH defects.

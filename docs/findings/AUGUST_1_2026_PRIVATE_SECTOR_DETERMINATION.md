# August 1, 2026 — Private-Sector Determination

## Determination

Minnesota Session Laws 2026, Chapter 72 creates a direct operative restriction
on covered private actors beginning August 1, 2026.

## Covered capability

The statute targets automated or readily accessible services capable of
producing realistic nudified media involving an identifiable individual.

## Covered conduct

- service access;
- service download;
- service use;
- performance of nudification for a user; and
- advertising or promotion.

## Enforcement exposure

- Attorney-general enforcement;
- civil penalties up to $500,000 per unlawful access, download, or use;
- private civil action;
- enhanced compensatory damages;
- punitive damages;
- injunctions; and
- attorney fees and costs.

## August 1 determination

August 1, 2026 is the normalized 60-day Federal implementation deadline.

The actors directly obligated by this deadline are Federal officials and
Federal bodies.

AI developers are potential voluntary participants in the framework that
Federal officials are required to design.

## Separate conditional interval

Section 3(b)(ii) permits a participating developer to provide controlled
Federal access to a covered frontier model for up to 30 days before the
developer plans to release that model to other trusted partners.

This is an event-relative interval, not a universal calendar compliance
deadline.

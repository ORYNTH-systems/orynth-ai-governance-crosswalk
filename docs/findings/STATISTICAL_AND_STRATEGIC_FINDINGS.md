# ORYNTH AI Governance Crosswalk

## Statistical and Strategic Findings

### Corpus boundary

This analysis covers a bounded corpus of **34 atomic requirements**:

- Executive Order 14409: **25**
- Minnesota HF 1606 / Chapter 72: **9**

The analysis uses only the public ORYNTH Reference Architecture Version 1.0.0
and the public ORYNTH Architectural Audit Specification Version 1.0.0.

It is an architectural compatibility assessment, not a legal-compliance
certification.

## Source-level findings

- **Executive Order 14409** — 18 of 25 requirements architecturally relevant (72%); 7 outside scope; 0 gap.
- **Minnesota HF 1606 / Chapter 72** — 2 of 9 requirements architecturally relevant (22.22%); 6 outside scope; 1 gap.

The two source instruments perform materially different functions. Executive
Order 14409 contains federal implementation, benchmarking, classification,
consultation, controlled-access, cybersecurity, confidentiality, and workforce
functions. Minnesota HF 1606 / Chapter 72 directly restricts specified
nudification capabilities and establishes legal consequences.

Their August 1, 2026 events remain separate:

- the federal event is an implementation deadline for designated federal work;
- the Minnesota event is the effective date of direct statutory restrictions.

## Strategic release position

The strongest defensible public position is:

> The public ORYNTH Reference Architecture and Architectural Audit Specification
> contain evidence-backed mechanisms that are directly or meaningfully relevant
> to selected emerging AI-governance requirements, especially in execution
> assurance, evidence, traceability, validation, reproducibility, auditability,
> authority preservation, and scope control.

The public release must immediately pair that statement with the following
boundary:

> The crosswalk is an architectural compatibility assessment. It does not
> establish legal compliance, government approval, regulatory certification,
> implementation certification, safety, correctness, or statutory satisfaction.

3. Distinguish direct support from related mechanisms.
4. Preserve every residual duty.
5. explain NOT_APPLICABLE as an explicit scope boundary.

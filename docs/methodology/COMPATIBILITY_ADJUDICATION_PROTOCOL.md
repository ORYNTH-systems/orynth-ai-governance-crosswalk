# Compatibility Adjudication Protocol

## Deterministic evidence derivation

Supporting evidence is derived from the actual PASS 04A evidence index.

The process is:

1. retrieve the normalized types assigned to a source requirement;
2. retrieve all PASS 04A evidence mapped to those types;
3. prioritize direct evidence classes;
4. select a bounded evidence set;
5. preserve residual legal and technical duties.

No evidence identifier is assumed or fabricated.

## Compatibility boundary

Compatibility is architectural only.

No verdict establishes:

- legal compliance;
- government approval;
- safety;
- correctness;
- deployment certification;
- statutory satisfaction;
- fitness for purpose.

## Denominators

Two denominators remain separate:

1. all source requirements;
2. architecturally applicable requirements excluding NOT_APPLICABLE.

Outside-scope legal remedies, staffing, appropriations, courts, penalties, and
sovereign enforcement are not counted as ORYNTH architectural gaps.

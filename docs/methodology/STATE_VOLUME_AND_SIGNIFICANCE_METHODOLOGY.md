# State Volume and Governance-Significance Methodology

## Purpose

This methodology prevents three different questions from being collapsed into
one misleading ranking.

## Measurement 1 — Raw legislative volume

Raw legislative volume means the number of introduced measures associated
with a jurisdiction during a defined legislative period.

A valid raw-volume calculation must state whether it includes:

- bills;
- resolutions;
- companion measures;
- appropriations;
- study measures;
- carried-over bills;
- amended duplicates;
- election-deepfake measures;
- biometric or privacy measures; and
- other AI-adjacent legislation.

This release does not claim complete state-level raw counts because a
fully normalized export of the monthly NCSL database has not yet been
ingested and deduplicated.

## Measurement 2 — Enacted-law volume

Enacted-law volume counts measures that reached enacted or equivalent
operative status.

The current calculation is restricted to the selected PASS 02A backbone.

It is not a claim that each listed state enacted only one AI law.

## Measurement 3 — Governance significance

Governance significance measures structural characteristics rather than bill
quantity.

The current model scores:

1. architectural breadth;
2. developer duties;
3. deployer duties;
4. evidence and documentation;
5. enforcement strength.

Each dimension is scored from 1 through 5.

The score does not determine:

- whether a law is constitutionally valid;
- whether a law is economically desirable;
- whether a law is well drafted;
- whether a state is politically superior;
- whether compliance has been achieved; or
- whether ORYNTH satisfies the law.

## Source hierarchy

National annual figures are derived from NCSL summaries and the NCSL
Artificial Intelligence Legislation Database.

Individual legal records remain controlled by official state and federal
sources.

## Incomplete-year rule

No final 2026 national or state ranking may be published before the relevant
legislative year closes or the dataset is explicitly labeled as an
as-of-date snapshot.

## Authority Finding

The order principally commands Federal officials and Federal bodies.

Private AI-developer participation in the section 3 framework is expressly
voluntary.

Section 3(c) states that section 3 does not authorize mandatory governmental
licensing, preclearance, or permitting for the development, publication,
release, or distribution of new AI models.

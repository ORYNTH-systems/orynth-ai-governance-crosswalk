# Major Enacted AI-Law Source Record

## Primary-source requirement

Every registry record must point to an official government source.

Secondary summaries may be used for discovery but do not control legal status,
effective dates, operative language, or enforcement interpretation.

## Limitation

This pass establishes breadth across governance models.

Later passes will expand:

- the number of enacted laws;
- the number of jurisdictions;
- amendment histories;
- litigation status;
- agency rulemaking;
- implementation guidance; and
- atomic requirement extraction.

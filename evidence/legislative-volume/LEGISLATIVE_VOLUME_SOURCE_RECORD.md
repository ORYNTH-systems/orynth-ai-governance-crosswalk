## Evidence rule

NCSL controls the national trend summary and discovery layer.

Official state legislative records control:

- individual measure status;
- introduction dates;
- enactment dates;
- final statutory language;
- effective dates;
- amendments;
- enforcement;
- penalties.

## Current limitation

The active database is updated monthly.

Therefore, 2026 data must be labeled with an as-of date and may not be treated
as a completed annual total.

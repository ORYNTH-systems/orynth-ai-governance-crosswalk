# ORYNTH AI Governance Crosswalk

## Version

0.1.0 — Formation State

## Purpose

The ORYNTH AI Governance Crosswalk is an evidence-based research project
that maps United States artificial-intelligence legislation, enacted law,
executive action, regulatory guidance, and formal governance requirements
against publicly available ORYNTH architectural and audit artifacts.

The project is intentionally divided into two independent layers:

1. AI Governance Requirements Matrix
2. ORYNTH Compatibility Assessment

The legislative and regulatory corpus must remain neutral and independently
usable before any ORYNTH compatibility determination is made.

## Core Research Questions

1. Which AI-related bills have been introduced in the United States?
2. Which bills became enacted law?
3. When was each measure drafted, introduced, announced, passed, signed,
   filed, made effective, or assigned a compliance deadline?
4. Which states are producing the greatest legislative volume?
5. Which states are producing the most structurally significant AI laws?
6. What distinct obligations are imposed on developers, deployers,
   distributors, platforms, employers, agencies, and other regulated actors?
7. Which obligations are addressed by publicly available ORYNTH artifacts?
8. What architectural or evidentiary gaps remain?

## Evidence Rule

No legislative status, legal obligation, date, or compatibility assessment
may be treated as confirmed without a traceable source.

Primary government records control over summaries.
